Text classification for Alzheimer’s disease detection. She fine-tuned BERT-based transformers on spontaneous-speech transcripts and compared them with logistic regression on linguistic features from the ELFEN library. She then used Integrated Gradients to see which words drove the predictions, and tested cross-dataset generalization and subgroup robustness.
